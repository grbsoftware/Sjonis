One markup, two skins

Both columns are stamped from the same <template>. The only difference is data-skin="bevel" on the right-hand wrapper — no skeleton, theme or component rule differs. Change the theme below and both re-derive: the bevel's light and dark edges are mixed from whatever surface the theme supplies, so it is a grammar, not a picture of Windows 95.

bevel

skins/bevel.css

What to look at. Corners are square and no surface casts a soft shadow — depth is drawn with a light edge on two sides and a dark edge on the other two. Press a button and it goes down rather than changing colour. The input is cut into the page: its edges are the raised ones reversed. Focus is a dotted rectangle inside the control, not a ring around it. None of that is reachable from a theme at any setting.
