How it works

Three things, then it is out of your way.

  1. Point it and press record.

    There is no level to set, so there is no level to get wrong. The meter is there to tell you the room is alive, not to be ridden.

  2. Mark what mattered.

    One button drops a cue while you are still listening. Cues survive as chapter marks in the file, so the edit starts from your memory of the day rather than from a waveform.

  3. Pull the card.

    Plain WAV on plain exFAT. No app, no account, no sync — the files are on the card and the card is in your hand.

Before you ask

The five we get every week.

Is 32-bit float actually different, or is it marketing?
Different, and narrowly so. It does not improve the microphones or the preamps — it removes clipping and the noise floor as things you have to trade against each other in the moment. If you always have time to set a level, you will not notice.
Can I use it as a USB interface?
Yes, class-compliant, two in and two out. No driver on macOS, Windows or Linux.
What happens when you stop making it?
The files are plain WAV on plain exFAT and nothing needs our servers, so a recorder we discontinued still works exactly as it did. Schematics and firmware go public when a model is retired.
Is there an app?
No, and that is the feature. Pull the card.
Do you ship outside the EU?
To 41 countries. Duties are calculated at checkout rather than collected from you on the doorstep.
