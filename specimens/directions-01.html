How to use this

Pick the plate you'd want to look at every day — not the one that photographs best. These are five different arguments about attention: Graphite and Blueprint assume you are working in the tool for hours and want it to disappear; Oxide and Halo assume the interface should have a point of view; Vellum assumes reading matters more than operating.

Tell me which one (or which two to cross), and iteration 02 goes deep on it: the full token set, the layout skeletons beyond this one screen — split view, three-pane, command palette, settings, empty and error states — and the component inventory built out properly.
